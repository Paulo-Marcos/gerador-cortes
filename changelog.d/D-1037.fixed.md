- **The same cut no longer pays for its scenes twice.** While the raw clip's chain
  was generating scenes, Pós showed an empty list and a free "Gerar cenas"; after
  switching cuts and coming back, the button was free again too, and the server
  accepted the second request. Each generation costs about US$ 0.33 and takes 256 s
  at the median. The server now keeps an in-memory mark per cut, taken before the
  raw clip is committed, and refuses a second generation with a clear message,
  whichever of the two sources came first; if the click came first, the chain does
  not generate again. The mark clears on error, cancellation and restart. Pós, the
  cut's row and the status bar say "gerando cenas" without offering to generate,
  validate or render, including after coming back or reloading the page, and the
  new scenes appear by themselves.
