- **The Pós scene list reads as a plan.** Each scene says where it came from:
  from the AI and checked, needs checking (the source warnings from D-1018),
  manual, or struck. The same color and icon mark the scene in the timeline,
  with a legend, and screen readers hear the origin too. A scene becomes manual
  when you create it with "Gerar manual" or rewrite what an AI scene says (its
  text, type, number and so on); moving it in time, changing its look or typing
  its source does not count. Until D-1075, generating the scenes again still
  replaces the whole plan, manual scenes included: the label says only
  "manual" and promises nothing more.
- **Striking a scene instead of deleting it.** The trash button became "Riscar".
  The struck scene stays in the list, crossed out, with "Trazer de volta", and
  stays out of the video: the render, the overlays and the editor preview skip
  it, the final review timeline no longer draws it, and its source warnings no
  longer count among the cut's pending items. Saving a struck or restored scene
  undoes the plan validation, like any other scene change.
- **"Marcar validadas" became "Validar o plano (faltam N)".** N counts the scenes
  that still need checking; the warnings never block validation. With no scenes
  the button stays disabled, as before (D-1070).
