- **Generating the scenes again keeps the previous round, to undo and compare.**
  A new generation used to replace the whole scene plan and the old one was lost.
  Now the round it replaces is kept, one only, with who generated it (Claude,
  Gemini or manual), when, and whether it was validated. `GET
  /api/cortes/{id}/cenas-remotion/rodadas` returns the current and the previous
  round with what came in, what went out and which fields changed in each scene;
  `POST .../rodadas/desfazer` swaps the two, so undoing again redoes. Undo holds
  the scenes' mark like a generation and refuses, writing nothing, if the scenes
  changed since the comparison (`versao`) or between its read and its write. The
  sync carries the previous round along, so an undo after cutting a stretch puts
  each scene back on its phrase. Backend only: the buttons come in D-1073
  (D-1071).
