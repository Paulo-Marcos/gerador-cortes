- **The Cuts step says what stays and what goes.** With a cut open, the Cuts
  stop on the step track reads "fica 17:43 · sai 2:28": the net length of the
  raw cut and the total its segments take out, in minutes and seconds, instead
  of "sai 148 s". A cut with no segment reads "fica 1:40 · nada sai". A segment
  inside another still counts once, and the open "N conferir" stays at the end
  (D-1090).
