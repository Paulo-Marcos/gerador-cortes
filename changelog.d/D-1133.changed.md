- **The app has three themes: Dark, Light and Auto.** The old palettes (Paper,
  Mist, Slate and the accent colors) are gone. A saved Slate or Dark theme
  opens as Dark, Paper, Mist or Light as Light, and with nothing saved the app
  now opens Dark (it used to open light). Auto follows the system and switches
  with it. The theme sits on the page itself, with the matching color scheme and
  browser-bar color.
