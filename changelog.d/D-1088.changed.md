- **Strike a cut instead of deleting it.** In the cut editor, `R` (and the
  new "Riscar corte" button next to "Approve" in the bottom bar) strikes the
  cut right away, with no dialog, and moves to the next cut. A struck cut stays
  in the database as `rejeitado`, keeping the AI proposal and everything the
  operator adjusted; its heavy media (raw clip, grade, overlays, renders) is
  released with the same rule as the project cleanup, so a Fire with pending
  shorts keeps what it still has to publish. The notice that follows offers
  the optional reason (the channel's chips) and Undo — the same "bring back"
  the struck list will use — which returns the cut to approved (if it was
  approved or rendered) or proposed, and regenerates the raw clip on its own. A cut already on YouTube cannot be struck: the button
  stays dimmed with the hint "Já está no YouTube". "Excluir corte" left the
  editor's More menu, and the telemetry counts a struck cut as deleted, with
  its reason.
