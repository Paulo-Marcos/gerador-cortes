  editor's Decisões tab, "1 · Tirar o silêncio? Sai X", sums every silence the
  app found (pauses of 0.8 s or more). One answer covers them all: "Tirar todos"
  takes them all out, "Manter todos" crosses them all out, each in a single
  request. Every silence that leaves shows the splice (SAI/FICA, with "pausa,
  sem fala" when nothing is said inside it) and its own "Riscar", the way to keep
  one as an exception. Question 2 no longer lists silences or counts them in its
  total. A crossed-out silence now stays crossed out: generating the raw clip
  again used to put it back, in both lists at once. The 0.8 s threshold and the
  0.5 s deduplication are unchanged (Paulo's decision P9, 08/11/2026; RN-36)
  (D-1093).
