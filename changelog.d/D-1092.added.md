- **Struck cuts at the end of the cut list, with "Trazer de volta".** In the
  cut editor, a struck cut no longer sits among the live cuts, and `J`/`K`
  skip it. It moves to a "Riscados · N" section at the end of "Cortes da live"
  (also in the cut picker of a narrow window), with its number and title
  struck through, the reason chips when there is a reason, the line "Proposta
  e trabalho guardados; a mídia saiu." and the button "Trazer de volta (regera
  o bruto)", which returns the cut to its previous stage and regenerates the
  raw clip on its own. A reason picked in the notice after `R` shows up in the
  section right away. In Post-production and Final review, which share the same
  cut list, struck cuts only drop out of the list and of `J`/`K`, with no
  section: bringing a cut back is a curation call. The "next cut" start shown
  in the editor skips struck cuts too. The cut API now returns
  `motivo_riscado`.
