- **"Juntar com o próximo" strikes the second cut instead of deleting it, with
  no browser confirm.** Joining two cuts no longer asks through the browser's
  `confirm`, and the second cut is no longer deleted: it becomes a struck cut,
  keeping its title, Fire, approval and the AI proposal, and its media is
  released as in "Limpar" (a pending Fire keeps its media). The notice "Cortes
  juntados." offers "Trazer de volta", which returns that cut to its previous
  stage and regenerates its raw clip; the joined cut keeps the merged span, so
  a full undo is "Trazer de volta" plus "Dividir" at the join. A struck cut is
  no longer anyone's neighbour: "next cut" skips it, it does not block a join
  as a cut in between, and it cannot be joined. It does not count as a deleted
  proposal in the cut telemetry. (D-1097)
