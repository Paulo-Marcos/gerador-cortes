- **The scene list in Post gets its height back.** Each step of the Post redesign
  (D-1070 to D-1075) stacked a fixed block above the scene list, and in a common
  900 px window only two scenes fit. What is reference now folds away and nothing
  is lost: the editorial opening shows one line and opens on click, the origin
  legend sits behind "Legenda" at the end of the numbers line and opens over the
  list, and the round fits in one line with "Desfazer" and a compare icon (the
  reason a disabled button is off stays visible). The channel filter is a chip in
  the tab row instead of a fixed strip, and the action bar at the bottom no longer
  wraps into two rows: open items shrink with an ellipsis and the render reason
  gives way before the buttons. At 1520×900 the list goes from 2 to 5 scenes
  (D-1086).
