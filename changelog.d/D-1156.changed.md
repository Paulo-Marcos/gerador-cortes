- **Coding agents may now delete the database copies left in worktrees, while
  production and the main tree stay locked.** The deny list in
  `.claude/settings.json` blocked every `*.db` relative to the session folder.
  As a result, an agent could not remove the production copies it had placed in
  a worktree for screenshots, and those copies had already broken a ship. The
  same relative pattern never reached production from a worktree session.

  Production (`C:\PRD`) and the main tree's `instance/` are now denied by
  absolute paths, and so are the databases in its `backend`, `.guia` and
  `scratch` folders, including the SQLite `-wal` and `-shm` files. These rules
  also stop `rm`, `mv`, `cp` and `cat`. The `.db` files under
  `.claude/worktrees` are free.

  `.env`, `token.json` and `client_secrets.json` are now also denied on any
  drive, so the main tree's `backend/.env` is no longer readable from a worktree
  session. The relative secret rules, `~/.ssh` and `~/.aws` stay as they were
  (D-1156).
