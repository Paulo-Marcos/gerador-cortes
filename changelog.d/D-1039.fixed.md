- **Editing a live's transcript no longer leaves the project and its cuts out
  of step.** The new text waited in the same session as the batch that copies
  it to the cuts, so the first rollback (a cut with invalid data, or "database
  is locked") threw it away while the next cuts still saved it. With the lock,
  the screen even reported success. The transcript and the subtitle offset are
  now saved first, and only then copied to the cuts; a cut that fails stays as
  it was and the error still reaches the operator. Adjusting the subtitle sync
  also stopped answering 500 with `MissingGreenlet` after a lock, when the data
  had already been saved.
