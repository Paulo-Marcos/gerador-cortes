- **Generating the scenes again no longer deletes the manual ones.** Every AI
  generation that replaces a cut's scenes (Claude, Gemini through `agy` or the
  API, and the AI answer pasted in the manual modal) used to overwrite the whole
  plan, so a scene the operator created or rewrote (`manual: true`) vanished
  without notice. Now manual scenes stay as they are: text, times, type and the
  struck-out state. A new AI scene that overlaps a manual one is dropped (touching
  edges do not count, and a struck-out manual scene also blocks, since "Bring
  back" puts it on the video again); the others come in and the plan is sorted by
  start time. The AI scenes of the old plan are replaced, struck out or not. The
  previous round keeps the whole plan, manual scenes included, so undo returns to
  exactly what it was. In Post, the manual label now reads "manual · a IA não
  mexe", a short line says how many AI scenes were left out until the operator
  saves the plan, and the "generate again" confirmation no longer says manual edits
  are lost (D-1075).
