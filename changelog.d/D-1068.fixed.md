- **The release script counts every form of the breaking marker again.** After
  D-1067 only `**BREAKING**` and `BREAKING:` opened a breaking change, so an
  entry written as `**BREAKING:**` or `BREAKING CHANGE:` would have suggested a
  patch from 1.0 on. `bin/release.py` now reads the whole word BREAKING, in
  capitals, at the start of a line (with or without the list dash or bold); the
  word in the middle of a sentence, a quoted marker and `BREAKINGS` still do
  not count (D-1068).
